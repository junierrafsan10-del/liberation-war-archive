Oral History Interviews

Recorded conversations with those who experienced the Liberation War first-hand. Each interview is preserved in full length and accompanied by a transcript.

👤

Captain A. Rahim

Sector Commander, 1971

Captain Rahim led guerrilla operations in Sector 2. In this interview he describes the training camps in India, supply line challenges, and the moment news of surrender reached his unit.

"We had nothing but courage and the people's support. That was enough."

👤

Ferdousi Begum

Civilian / Refugee Camp Volunteer

Ferdousi worked in a refugee camp in West Bengal, caring for women and children displaced by the war. She recounts the humanitarian crisis and the resilience of the displaced.

"I saw a nation being reborn inside those tents."

👤

Shahidul Alam

Photojournalist

Shahidul documented the war as a young photographer. He talks about the risks of capturing images under fire and the iconic photographs that shaped global opinion.

"The camera was my weapon. The truth was my ammunition."
