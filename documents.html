Featured Document

Proclamation of Independence

Published: 26 March 1971 • Source: Bangladesh National Archives

The Proclamation of Independence was issued by Sheikh Mujibur Rahman in the early hours of 26 March 1971, following the Pakistani military’s crackdown (Operation Searchlight). The declaration read over radio by Major Ziaur Rahman on behalf of Mujib formally severed ties with West Pakistan and called upon the people of East Pakistan to resist.

This document is regarded as the founding legal instrument of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh. Its original Bangla text was broadcast from a clandestine transmitter; the English translation was circulated internationally through diplomatic channels.

  • Type: Declaration
  • Date: 26 March 1971
  • Medium: Radio broadcast transcript / manuscript
  • Source: Bangladesh National Archives, Dhaka — File No. 71/IND/001
