Preserving the Memory of the Liberation War

The Liberation War Archive is a digital repository dedicated to collecting, preserving, and sharing the documents, personal testimonies, and photographs that chronicle the struggle for independence. Explore firsthand accounts, official records, and a visual record of the nation's defining moment.

Our Mission

To ensure that the history of the Liberation War remains accessible, accurate, and alive for researchers, students, and the public. We collect, digitize, and contextualize primary sources so that future generations can understand the sacrifices made for freedom.
